# Part-of-Speech (POS)

## 1. Introduction

Part-of-Speech (POS) identifies the grammatical role of a word in a sentence.

The role of a word can depend on how it is used in the sentence.

Conceptually:

```text
Sentence
   ↓
POS Processing
   ↓
Words + Grammatical Roles
```

---

## 2. Common POS Categories

The following grammatical roles were covered:

- Noun
- Pronoun
- Verb
- Adjective
- Adverb
- Preposition
- Conjunction
- Determiner

---

## 3. Noun

A **noun** is a word that represents a person, place, thing, or idea.

Examples:

```text
boy
school
movie
story
```

Example:

```text
The boy went to school.
```

Here:

```text
boy    → Noun
school → Noun
```

---

## 4. Pronoun

A **pronoun** is used in place of a noun.

Examples:

```text
he
she
it
they
we
```

Example:

```text
Rahul went home because he was tired.
```

Here:

```text
he → Pronoun
```

---

## 5. Verb

A **verb** expresses an action or state.

Examples:

```text
run
study
enjoy
worked
```

Example:

```text
The boy ran to school.
```

Here:

```text
ran → Verb
```

---

## 6. Adjective

An **adjective** describes or provides information about a noun.

Examples:

```text
smart
beautiful
excellent
wonderful
```

Example:

```text
The smart boy studied.
```

Here:

```text
smart → Adjective
```

It describes the noun `boy`.

---

## 7. Adverb

An **adverb** provides information about a verb or describes how an action occurs.

Examples:

```text
quickly
slowly
carefully
```

Example:

```text
The boy quickly ran to school.
```

Here:

```text
quickly → Adverb
```

It describes how the boy ran.

---

## 8. Preposition

A **preposition** expresses a relationship between words, often involving location, direction, or other relationships.

Examples:

```text
to
in
on
at
```

Example:

```text
The boy ran to the school.
```

Here:

```text
to → Preposition
```

---

## 9. Conjunction

A **conjunction** connects words, phrases, or parts of a sentence.

Examples:

```text
and
but
or
```

Example:

```text
The boy studied and played.
```

Here:

```text
and → Conjunction
```

---

## 10. Determiner

A **determiner** helps specify a noun.

Examples:

```text
the
a
an
```

Example:

```text
The boy went to the school.
```

Here:

```text
The → Determiner
the → Determiner
```

---

## 11. Example: Complete POS Identification

Sentence:

```text
The smart boy quickly ran to the school and studied.
```

POS identification:

| Word | POS |
|---|---|
| The | Determiner |
| smart | Adjective |
| boy | Noun |
| quickly | Adverb |
| ran | Verb |
| to | Preposition |
| the | Determiner |
| school | Noun |
| and | Conjunction |
| studied | Verb |

---

## 12. Important Point

The grammatical role of a word should be determined from its use in the sentence.

POS processing is therefore concerned with identifying the role that a word plays in its context.

---

## 13. Summary

- **Noun** → person, place, thing, or idea
- **Pronoun** → used in place of a noun
- **Verb** → action or state
- **Adjective** → describes a noun
- **Adverb** → provides information about an action
- **Preposition** → expresses a relationship
- **Conjunction** → connects words or sentence parts
- **Determiner** → helps specify a noun
